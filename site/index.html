Handwriting Font Maker

by Jed Blenkhorn

Turn your handwriting into a font you can install and type with. Print a sheet, fill it in with a pen or pencil, then scan it or take a photo.

1. Get a sheet

Sheet
How will you fill it in?

For a class, paste in a list of names. Each person gets their own sheet with their name printed on it, and the whole pile can be scanned in one go.

Loading...

Print in black and white. Any dark pen or pencil works. Felt pen gives the cleanest result; with pencil, press firmly and rub out mistakes properly.

Illustrator: open the .ai. All the pages are side by side on one artboard, the sheet is on a locked layer, and there's an empty "Draw here" layer on top. Draw each character in black with the Paintbrush, Blob Brush or Pencil. Then save it as an .ai (Illustrator adds "[Converted]" to the name, that's fine) with Create PDF Compatible File ticked, which is on by default.

Procreate and other apps: import the PDF, draw on a new layer in black, then export the whole page as a PDF or PNG.

2. Make your font

Upload your filled-in sheets: scans, phone photos, PDFs, or the .ai file you drew on. You can add several at once, like both pages of a Standard sheet or a whole class. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, it all happens on this page.

About

Is it free? Yes. No account, no watermark, and the fonts are yours to use however you like.

Where do my sheets go? Nowhere. Reading the sheets and building the font all happen in your browser, so your handwriting never leaves your device.

Using it with a class? Paste the class list into Names, print the pile, and scan them all back in as one PDF. Everyone gets their own font, and you can download them together as a zip.

Something not working? Email hello@jedbillyb.com with the photo or scan that failed and I'll take a look.
